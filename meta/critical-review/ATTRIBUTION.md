# Attribution

## The checklist

The seven dimensions and their questions come from a practitioner's working checklist, **"Critical
Review Questions — A Co-Intelligence Checklist for Partnering with AI"** (shared 2026-07-28) — the same
derived working notes credited in the sibling skill `co-intelligence`'s ATTRIBUTION. The framing they
distill is Ethan Mollick's *Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI* (Portfolio, 2024); the
questions themselves are the practitioner's operational derivation, not the book's text, and should not
be attributed to Mollick.

## What is original here

- The **workflow** (ground → challenge → evidence → report) and the requirement to verify checkable
  claims against ground truth rather than grading the artifact's prose.
- The **report structure** (covered/gaps per dimension, decision-ordered action list) — distilled from
  applying the checklist to a real production doc set (an OTA update platform's technical docs), where
  the covered/gaps split kept strengths from being regressed and the capped action list is what made the
  review actionable.
- The **pitfalls table**, including "polishing instead of challenging" and "reviewing only the text".
- The gloss lines explaining *why* each question earns its place.

## Division of labor with `co-intelligence`

`co-intelligence` is the stance: whether and how to hand work to AI, and the self-review prompt run on
one's own output. `critical-review` is the applied procedure: the checklist run against a specific
artifact, producing a findings report for its owner. The two share provenance and are deliberately
non-overlapping in trigger.
