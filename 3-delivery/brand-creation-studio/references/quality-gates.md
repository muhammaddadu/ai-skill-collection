# Identity quality gates

Apply these as loop-back gates, not as presentation checklists. A schedule or complete-looking deck is not evidence that the identity is ready.

## Gate 1: shortlist eligibility

Review with the [creative review rubric](creative-review-rubric.md). A route does not advance when it has any unresolved critical failure:

- obvious category cliché without an ownable transformation
- weak or inaccurate typography
- dependence on mockup atmosphere or colour for recognition
- no wider system potential
- indistinct performance at small sizes
- resemblance to a competitor or generic generated-brand template

Shortlist no more than two or three routes. If none passes, return to the visual lab rather than manufacturing a winner.

## Gate 2: final-route proof

The leading route must have at least two visible refinement iterations and pass:

- cliché contradiction check against `cliche-red-list.md`
- silhouette and memory check
- competitor-swap check
- no-logo recognition check
- monochrome and one-colour check
- 16/24 px check where relevant
- accessibility and contrast checks
- low-cost or constrained-production check
- one ordinary document or UI application
- one expressive brand moment

Iteration notes must identify concrete changes to geometry, spacing, typography, colour, composition, or system logic. “Make it cleaner” is not a rationale.

If a competitor's logo could replace the final logo while the rest of the system still looked plausible, the route is not distinctive enough. If removing the logo erases recognition, strengthen the typography, colour behaviour, imagery, and composition before continuing.

## Gate 3: production readiness

Before calling the identity final, verify:

- final marks and wordmarks are accurate vector or deterministic artwork
- at least three recognition assets exist beyond the logo
- logo family, responsive variants, clear space, and minimum sizes are defined
- named typography, licences, colour roles, and accessible combinations are documented
- flat comps prove tiny digital, core product/conversion, formal, expressive, environmental, and monochrome contexts as relevant
- mockups composite accurate artwork rather than generated approximations
- source files, exports, and `asset-manifest.md` agree
- final red-team review has no unresolved generic default

Loop back whenever a critical check fails. Do not finish cosmetically.
